What works and what doesn't

Straight answers, so you know which mode to use.

Background never touches focus or your mouse. Roblox takes it for buttons and menus, but it can ignore clicks into the 3D world, because those only come from the real mouse while Roblox is in focus.

Focus bounce gives Roblox focus for a few tens of milliseconds, clicks for real, and hands focus straight back to your game. It's the reliable one. Borderless and windowed games handle it fine; a game in exclusive fullscreen may flicker or minimise.

Your pad. Pad input goes to whatever has focus, so Split waits for the pad to go quiet before a bounce. The pad lane reads Xbox-style pads on Windows; PlayStation pads show up through Steam Input or DS4Windows. macOS has no background pad reading.

Safety. Split never opens, reads or injects into Roblox; it sends the same input Windows or macOS would. No admin needed. Some games forbid AFK automation in their own rules.
